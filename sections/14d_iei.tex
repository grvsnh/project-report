\section*{IEI PROJECT SUBMISSION}
The Institution of Engineers (India) is the national professional body of
engineers, founded in 1919 and headquartered in Kolkata. Incorporated by Royal
Charter in 1935, it is the largest multidisciplinary organisation of engineers in
the country, and it represents the profession across every branch of engineering,
including computer science and the allied disciplines. Through its national and
local centres, its student chapters, and its technical divisions, the Institution
organises seminars, conventions and continuing-education programmes, publishes
technical journals, and upholds the professional standards and code of ethics of
the engineering profession in India.

VeerDrishti has now been submitted to the Institution of Engineers (India) as an
entry under its student project programme. The project report, the abstract, and
the institutional certificates were filed with the application. The entry is now
with the Institution for evaluation, and its further response is awaited.

\textbf{Status: application submitted; response awaited.}
